Bill asked: "who is the editor-in-chief for Red Hat skill offerings in
general?" Nobody owns this role.

Peter: "I'm definitely not the editor-in-chief for Red Hat skills...
there is no governing body over the top and everybody is building out
theirs." Peter is the gatekeeper for getting skills onto OpenShift AI
(the "closed version") but not the portfolio-wide authority.

**Same pattern as MCP:** "everybody was kind of doing their own thing
instead of having one standard around those. And it came back to bite
us when we tried to find a standard for including it into the
OpenShift AI area."

**Practical impact:** when trying to include skills into RHOAI,
standards misalignment across teams creates integration friction.
Multiple teams producing skills (OCP5, RHAI, UIE, partnership
ecosystem) with no unified governance on format, quality bar, or
distribution path.
