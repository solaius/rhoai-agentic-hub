Red Hat Ecosystem Catalog's AI section. Three content types:

**Agentic Skill Packs** (4 listed): Red Hat customers, SRE, OpenShift,
OpenShift Virtualization -- all under "Site Reliability" category.

**Agentic Skills** (2 listed + "coming soon"): RHEL Best Practices,
RHEL Translator -- under "Administration" category.

**MCP Servers** (5 listed + "coming soon"): RHEL, OpenShift, Ansible
Automation Platform, Lightspeed, Satellite -- under "Administration".

MCP servers ship as container images through Red Hat's certified container
catalog. Skills ship as SKILL.md files installable in Claude Code, Cursor,
and OpenShift Dev Spaces.

Subscription-gated: all assets tied to Red Hat subscriptions.

Relevant to both skills-catalog (skill packs + skills) and mcp-catalog
(MCP servers).
