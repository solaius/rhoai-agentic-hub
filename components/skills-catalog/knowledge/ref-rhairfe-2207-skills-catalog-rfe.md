The customer-facing RFE for the core skills catalog capability:
enabling RHOAI users to discover, explore, and acquire enterprise-
approved skills through a centralized catalog in AI Hub.

Status: Approved. Fix version: 3.5 GA RHOAI RELEASE.

This is the RFE counterpart of the RHAISTRAT-1780 Feature. Multiple
enterprise accounts and ISV partners validated the need for a
self-service skill discovery experience with trust-tier visibility
before acquisition.
