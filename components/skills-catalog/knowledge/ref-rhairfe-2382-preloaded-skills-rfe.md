The customer-facing RFE for pre-loaded skills: ship the catalog with
curated Red Hat and ISV partner skills so AI engineers have immediate
access to production-ready skills without manual registration.

Status: Approved. No fix version set.

This is the RFE counterpart of RHAISTRAT-1940. References the EX
lightforge project as the Red Hat skills source and the Partnership
Ecosystem team for ISV partner skill sourcing. Multiple named accounts
validated the empty-catalog problem.
